How To Build Your Own AI (0.2.1 Beta)

A browser model lab for building, teaching, training from scratch, and testing local models

1. What Changed in 0.2.1 Beta?

  • Training in a Web Worker (0.2.1): training runs in the background, so the page stays smooth on phones and the Stop button stops right away, keeping the epochs already finished.
  • Mini-batch training (0.2.1): the engine now learns from batches of examples with flatter loops, which is about twice as fast on the same hardware and trains more stably.
  • Binary model storage (0.2.1): weights are saved as raw Float32 data instead of JSON text, so saved models are much smaller. Older saves upgrade automatically on the first save.
  • Last saved + backup reminder (0.2.1): the header shows when your work was last saved. Tap it, or the reminder bar, to export everything (models, datasets, preferences and notes) in one file.
  • Studio Assistant side panel: a Copilot-style chat docked on the right (toggle with the header button or Alt+A, drag its edge to resize) for the 3 local assistants. Chat Studio is now just for your own trained models.
  • Search my documents: the assistant can answer from files in your Corpus Vault and the active model's memory, with numbered source chips under each reply.
  • Q&A Generator: in Train Studio, turn paragraphs into user:/ai: pairs (instant rules or assistant-written) and multiply them with paraphrased questions.
  • Speed benchmark: in Evaluation, compare tokens per second, latency and first-token time across your models and assistants, with a chart and JSON export.
  • Streaming and speed stats: replies stream live with a Stop button and show t/s, token count and response time.
  • Do-it-for-me actions (0.2.0): ask the assistant in plain words to create a model, train it, evaluate, clean or check the corpus, export the model, or switch tabs, and the app performs the action and reports back.
  • Read my screen (0.2.0): capture a window or tab (or OCR a screenshot you paste or pick), then say "turn this into training data" to convert the text into user:/ai: pairs.
  • Corpus pre-flight + auto-fix + undo (0.2.0): find duplicates, conflicting answers and short answers before training, fix them in one click, export Q&A pairs as CSV, and undo the last training run to restore previous weights.
  • Chat scrolls properly: the app fills the screen and only the message list scrolls.

# Dialogue Format (Best for chat & greetings)
