How To Train & Manage Your Pure JS AI

Mastering formatting, anti-repetition tuning, and multi-device setup

1. How to Format Training Text

Your model learns by reading dialogue transitions. Always format training data as clean User: and AI: turns:

User: Hello!
AI: Hi there! How can I help you today?
User: What is pure JavaScript?
AI: Pure JavaScript means running code without external libraries or servers.

2. Fixing Loops & Repetition

If your model starts repeating the same sentence (e.g. "hello im good how are you hello im good..."):

  • Increase Repetition Penalty: Set the slider between 1.2 and 1.5 in Chat Studio.
  • Lower Max Tokens: Keep response limits around 30-50 tokens for short Q&A.
  • Use Stop Words: The AI engine automatically cuts off generation as soon as it sees User: or a double newline.
  • Use Correct / Teach: Click the yellow graduation cap under a repeating response, type the ideal short answer, and save it!

3. Syncing to Phone & GitHub Pages

Because this app runs locally inside browser storage, trained models do not automatically sync across different devices over the cloud:

  1. Train your model on your PC where pasting text is easiest.
  2. Go to Models Hub and click Export Active Model (.json).
  3. Send that JSON file to your phone.
  4. Open your live GitHub Pages link on your phone (https://username.github.io/repo/) and click Import Model JSON!
