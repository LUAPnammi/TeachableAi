How To Train & Tune Your Browser AI (v3.0)

Why v3.0 stops repetition loops and how to combine Q&A pairs with raw paragraphs

1. What Changed in the v3.0 Trainer?

In v2.0, typing hi only fed the single word hi into the neural network without the user: ... ai: turn markers it saw during training, causing it to drift into random sentences.

  • Automatic Turn Conditioning: When you type hi in Chat Studio, the engine automatically formats the internal context as user: hi <sep> ai: so the neural network knows it must output an answer!
  • Semantic TF-IDF Memory: Both engines index every Q&A pair and paragraph sentence using TF-IDF cosine similarity, blending exact recall with neural generation.
  • Top-K + Repetition Penalty: Filters out low-probability gibberish tokens before sampling.

2. Mixing Q&A Pairs + Knowledge Paragraphs

You can combine both formats in the same training session:

# Dialogue Format (Best for chat & greetings)
user: hi
ai: Hello! How can I help you today?
# Paragraph Format (Best for facts & essays)
Photosynthesis is the process used by plants to convert light energy into chemical energy.
